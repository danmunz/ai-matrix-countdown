3b Mechanics — the decisions that keep it from feeling janky.
SEVEN RULES · ALL IMPLEMENTED IN 3a
01Non-linear geometry. Tick spacing follows the story, not the calendar: entries cluster where the years compress. Position = 2 + (i/47)1.15 × 96.
02Magnet field, not snapping. Ticks within ~7% of the handle grow up to 22px and brighten. You feel the entry before you land on it — no sudden jump.
03Handle follows the finger exactly while dragging (transition: none). On release it eases to the nearest tick over 150ms. Lag during drag is the #1 source of jank.
04The readout never lies. It always shows the day you would land on, changing only as you cross a tick — so pressing, holding and releasing never move the number by surprise. The entry itself — title, part, ordinal, body — waits until you let go, then the title decodes in with the landing page's exact ghost-phrase scramble (same glyph set, 42ms frame, 2 chars per frame). Numbers reward the drag, text never strobes.
05Relative dragging, never a jump. Pressing lifts the handle where it already stands; it only moves by how far the pointer travels, so a click can't teleport the reader to another decade. Pointer capture keeps the drag alive outside the bar.
06Reserved height for the headline (104px min) so a one-line title and a two-line title don't shove the page while you scrub.
07The handle is an instrument, not a swatch. Grained red readout plate (the BEGIN AT TODAY film grain, Geist Semibold numerals) over a notched pointer and a ringed bearing: black disc, red ring, white core, lit by a red bloom. Hover brightens the bloom and lifts the bearing 12%; grabbing swells it 22% and releases two slow concentric ripples. Idle is perfectly still.

Five trends are already underway in 2026. Each is separately reasonable — a cheaper way to buy compute, a fairer way to own it, a medical breakthrough, a better interface, a manager that never sleeps. None of them is a conspiracy. None of them requires a machine that hates you.
The story is what happens when they interlock: compute becomes a household commodity, then an asset class, then a biological one, then the place people live, and finally something no person is left administering. Follow any one and it looks like progress. Follow all five and it looks like August 7 2068.
